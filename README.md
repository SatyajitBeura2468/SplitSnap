## What is SplitSnap?

**Split the bill. Not the friendship.** SplitSnap makes shared expenses easier to manage, from a one-off meal to a group trip. It calculates shares, tracks who paid, and turns the math into a simple settle-up plan.

## Features

- **Quick Split:** divide a bill, add a tip, round each share, and share the result.
- **Groups & expenses:** track friends, categories, multiple payers, notes, and dates.
- **Flexible splitting:** equal shares, custom ratios, percentages, fixed amounts, or itemized bills with tax and tip.
- **Smart settle-up:** see balances, suggested payments, and partial payments.
- **History & recurring costs:** search and filter past expenses; schedule weekly or monthly expenses.
- **Backup & restore:** export your data and bring it back when needed.
- **Light / dark themes** and a built-in calculation self-test.

## Get started

1. [**Open SplitSnap**](https://splitsnap-teamsatyajitbeura.vercel.app/).
2. Choose **Quick** for a one-time bill or **Groups** to track expenses over time.
3. Use **More → Backup** to save a copy of your records.

You can also download this repository and open `index.html` in a modern browser. No build step or installation is required.

## Your data stays on your device

SplitSnap stores groups, expenses, and settings in your browser's **IndexedDB**. There is no account, app server, or cloud sync, so your data does not automatically move between devices. Clearing this site's browser data can erase saved records. Keep a backup using **More → Backup**.

## Built with

Single-file **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript**. Calculations use integer paise to avoid floating-point money errors; the app includes a built-in self-test for key allocation and settlement cases.
